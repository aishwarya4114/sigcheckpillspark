How SigCheck works

SigCheck turns pharmacy faxes into reviewable draft orders, and every field must pass checks the AI cannot override.

Design decisions

The model reads; it never decides

Nicrron returns verbatim text and leaves anything unsure empty. Every field must then pass deterministic checks it can't influence.

Never guess, never auto-fix

"5.0 mg" is flagged, not corrected. A missing value stays blank. A drug-name typo gets suggestions, never a substitution.

Safety screen before any model

Text that tries to instruct the software blocks the whole document before extraction. PRISM records it; our local check enforces it.

Three kinds of issue, three resolutions

Transcription doubts are confirmed or corrected against the fax. Prescriber clarifications need the prescriber's unambiguous answer. Blocked documents are quarantined.

Rules as versioned data, safety locked on

An administrator can tighten review policy and re-run checks deterministically. Injection blocking, required fields, NPI checks and human approval can't be switched off.

Minimum-necessary telemetry

OCR runs on the device. PRISM receives statuses and issue codes with identifiers masked. The model call is the one disclosure, and production needs a BAA or a self-hosted model.
